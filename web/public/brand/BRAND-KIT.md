# Hypescript brand kit

- Mark: `hypescript-mark.svg` — one rounded H/play glyph inside a calm squircle. Flat, minimal and readable at favicon size.
- Product raster: `hypescript-mark-minimal.png`; use this cache-safe master in the app UI.
- Wordmarks: `hypescript-wordmark-light.svg` and `hypescript-wordmark-dark.svg`.
- Product cards: `products/creator.svg`, `products/pro.svg`.
- Primary navy: `#0B1733`.
- Primary emerald: `#35D59A`.
- Wordmark descriptor: `AI VIDEO EDITOR`; use the horizontal mark wherever there is enough width.
- Product tone: calm, direct, editorial; communicate AI through capability and motion, never robots, sparkles or generic AI gradients.
- Minimum mark size: 20px in product UI and 16px only for browser favicons. Prefer the full lockup whenever 160px of width is available.
