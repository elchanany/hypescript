# Hypescript brand kit

- Mark: `hypescript-mark.svg` — navy app tile with one emerald forward/play ribbon.
- Wordmarks: `hypescript-wordmark-light.svg` and `hypescript-wordmark-dark.svg`.
- Product cards: `products/creator.svg`, `products/pro.svg`.
- Primary navy: `#0B1733`.
- Primary emerald: `#35D59A`.
- Product tone: calm, direct, editorial; avoid robots, sparkles and generic AI gradients.
- Minimum mark size: 16px. Do not add text inside the mark or place it on a busy image.
