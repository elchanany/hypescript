# Hypescript — Brand Guidelines

## Core idea

The mark is a Brain + Play `H`: two mirrored, abstract brain rails form the letter while a separate rounded play triangle becomes its visual crossbar. It connects AI and video editing without robots, sparkles or generic circuit imagery.

## Canonical assets

- `/brand/sources/hypescript-brain-play-master.png` — approved raster source master.
- `/brand/hypescript-mark-v4.png` — canonical compact product mark.
- `/brand/hypescript-logo-light.png` — horizontal raster lockup for light surfaces.
- `/brand/hypescript-logo-dark.png` — horizontal raster lockup for dark surfaces.
- Both wordmarks include `AI VIDEO EDITOR` when there is enough width.
- Raster icons, favicon, PWA icons and social cards are deterministically generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.py`.

Do not reference asset paths directly in components. Use `BrandLogo` and `BRAND_PATHS` from `web/lib/brand/assets.ts`.

## Usage

| Context | Asset |
|---|---|
| Editor top bar, compact sidebar, favicon and PWA | Mark |
| Landing page, Login, Dashboard, Onboarding, Account and legal headers | Horizontal wordmark |
| Open Graph and social sharing | Generated social card |
| Subscription product cards | Product SVG with current mark |

Minimum mark size is 20px in product UI; 16px is reserved for favicons. Minimum horizontal wordmark height is 28px. Preserve clear space of roughly one eighth of the mark width.

## Palette

- Navy `#0B1733` — app tile and deep background.
- Emerald `#35D59A` — timeline rails and primary brand accent.
- Accessible emerald on light surfaces `#159B70`.
- White `#F7FAFF` — playhead and dark-surface typography.
- Muted `#9AA7BC` — descriptor on dark surfaces.

## Accessibility and metadata

- Non-decorative `BrandLogo` uses `Hypescript` as alt text.
- Page metadata identifies the product as an AI video editor.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, PNG favicons, `favicon.ico`, Apple icon, maskable icons and social images must be regenerated after any mark change.
- Do not use the horizontal wordmark as a favicon or repeat alt text beside visible product text.
